1. Scope and the People Covered

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed by NextAppt.ai in connection with its United States-facing websites, appointment-search services, consumer accounts, appointment alerts, business listings, business accounts and dashboards, integrations, widgets, analytics, subscriptions, communications, and future features that link to this Policy. For clarity:
  • Consumer Users are people who search, browse, create consumer accounts, request alerts, save preferences, follow booking links, submit reviews or feedback, or use consumer-facing features.
  • Business Users are owners, employees, contractors, practitioners, or other authorized representatives who claim, verify, manage, advertise, integrate, or subscribe on behalf of a business.
A person may fall into both categories.

2. Our Role and Independent Businesses

NextAppt.ai operates an appointment discovery and technology platform. Businesses listed on NextAppt.ai are generally independent organizations with their own privacy practices. When you leave NextAppt.ai, use a third-party booking system, submit information directly to a provider, or receive a service from a provider, that provider or third party may independently collect and use information under its own privacy policy. Where a business connects an integration or supplies data to NextAppt.ai, the business is responsible for having the rights, notices, and consents required to provide that information.

3. Information We Collect From Consumer Users

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:
  • name, email address, telephone number, account identifier, login and verification information;
  • saved businesses, favorite services, appointment preferences, preferred dates or times, preferred locations, notification settings, and marketing preferences;
  • search queries, viewed businesses, viewed appointments, clicked booking links, alert requests, booking-referral activity, and interactions with search results;
  • reviews, ratings, feedback, messages, support requests, profile content, and other information you submit;
  • transaction, deposit, booking, cancellation, or payment-related information if those features are introduced; and
  • other information you choose to provide.
Searches for wellness, massage, healthcare-adjacent, beauty, fitness, or similar services may reveal or suggest sensitive interests. Do not enter medical records, diagnoses, treatment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in general search or feedback fields.

4. Information We Collect From Business Users

For Business Users we may collect:
  • name, work email, telephone number, job title or role, business affiliation, and account identifiers;
  • business-claim and verification information, including evidence of ownership, employment, authorization, domain control, professional credentials, or identity where reasonably necessary;
  • business profile, listing, service, price, staff, practitioner, availability, address, location, logo, image, promotion, and contact information;
  • subscription plan, billing contact, transaction, and payment-related information;
  • booking-platform, CRM, calendar, API, widget, or other integration configuration;
  • API keys, tokens, site IDs, location IDs, or other credentials submitted to connect an integration, which we treat as confidential security information;
  • analytics, lead, click, referral, and account-activity information; and
  • support, verification, dispute, and other communications.

5. Location Information

With permission from your browser, device, or application, NextAppt.ai may collect precise geolocation such as latitude and longitude. We may also receive or infer approximate location, city, metropolitan area, or region from information such as an IP address. We may use location to show nearby businesses and appointments, calculate distance, rank search results, center maps, identify relevant markets, reduce fraud, secure accounts, and improve the Services. You may deny or revoke device-location permission through your browser or device settings. Some proximity features may work less accurately without precise location. Where precise geolocation is treated as sensitive personal information, we process it in accordance with applicable law.

6. Information Collected Automatically

When you use NextAppt.ai, we or our service providers may automatically collect technical and usage information such as:
  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • device type and operating system;
  • device or browser identifiers;
  • language and approximate region;
  • referring and exit pages;
  • pages, searches, links, buttons, and appointment cards viewed or clicked;
  • dates, timestamps, session information, crash data, performance data, and security logs; and
  • cookie, local-storage, or similar technology identifiers.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NextAppt.ai and service providers may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs, session technologies, and similar tools for authentication, security, account persistence, preferences, maps, functionality, analytics, performance, attribution, fraud prevention, and—if introduced—advertising or personalization. Your browser may allow you to delete or block cookies or manage site permissions. Blocking certain technologies may prevent account or platform features from working. If NextAppt.ai introduces technologies that require consent or an opt-out mechanism, we will provide the legally required controls.

8. Information From Businesses, Integrations, Public Sources, and Other Third Parties

We may receive information from listed businesses, booking platforms, CRMs, calendars, payment processors, maps, geolocation services, email providers, analytics providers, identity or fraud-prevention providers, referral sources, public websites, public business directories, professional listings, public social-media pages, and other lawful sources. Business listing information can include business names, addresses, phone numbers, websites, booking URLs, coordinates, hours, service menus, durations, prices, staff or practitioner names, appointment availability, logos, images, descriptions, specialties, amenities, reviews, offers, and other business information.

9. Inferences, AI, and Automated Processing

NextAppt.ai may derive or infer information such as likely service interests, preferred areas, search intent, recommended appointments, inferred appointment availability, duplicate records, business relevance, fraud or security signals, or likely user preferences. We may use artificial intelligence or machine-learning systems for search assistance, recommendations, business-content tools, support, classification, availability inference, fraud detection, analytics, and product development. AI-generated or inferred outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate. We do not intend to use automated systems to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about consumers without providing notices and rights required by applicable law.

We may use personal information to:
  • provide appointment search, maps, distance calculations, business profiles, accounts, alerts, booking links, dashboards, integrations, widgets, analytics, and support;

  • send requested alerts and other transactional or relationship communications;
  • send marketing communications where permitted and subject to applicable choices;
  • measure, test, maintain, debug, develop, and improve NextAppt.ai;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, prohibited conduct, and technical problems;
  • enforce agreements and protect NextAppt.ai, users, businesses, and others; and
  • comply with law, legal process, accounting obligations, tax requirements, and legitimate legal claims.

11. How We Disclose Personal Information

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients as reasonably necessary:

Businesses and providers

When a Consumer User directs an interaction with a business, clicks through to booking, submits a request, books or pays through a feature, or otherwise asks us to connect the parties, relevant information may be disclosed to the business.

Service providers

We may use vendors for hosting, databases, cloud infrastructure, email, communications, maps, geolocation, analytics, security, customer support, identity verification, fraud prevention, payment processing, and professional services.

Booking and integration partners

We may exchange information with booking platforms, CRMs, calendars, APIs, widgets, and other connected services to retrieve availability, synchronize business information, facilitate booking, or provide requested integrations.

Payment and transaction parties

If payment features are offered, information may be disclosed to payment processors, banks, card networks, fraud-prevention providers, and the business involved in the transaction.

Analytics, attribution, and advertising partners

We may disclose device, usage, approximate location, and interaction information to analytics or attribution providers. If we introduce targeted advertising or cross-context advertising disclosures, we will update this Policy and provide legally required choices.

Business account administrators

Authorized administrators of a business account may be able to view business-account users, billing information, analytics, leads, profile activity, and integration information associated with that business.

Legal, safety, and corporate transactions

We may disclose information to comply with law, respond to legal process, investigate fraud or misconduct, protect rights or safety, establish or defend legal claims, or in connection with financing, formation of an operating entity, merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar transaction.

At your direction

We may disclose information when you direct us to or otherwise provide legally valid authorization.

23. Children

NextAppt.ai is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13. Consumer and Business accounts are intended for individuals who are at least 18. An adult may use the platform to locate or arrange a provider service for a minor where appropriate, but should not submit unnecessary personal information about the minor. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 in circumstances requiring parental consent and did not obtain that consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information. A parent or guardian may contact privacy@nextappt.ai.
